## 3. Full Description

```text
Flashlet is a lightweight, fast, and intuitive flashcard app designed for students, language learners, exam preparation, and anyone who wants to memorize information efficiently.

Master new vocabulary, terms, definitions, and concepts one card at a time with smooth tap-to-flip digital flashcards and an interface built entirely around active recall.

WHY CHOOSE FLASHLET?
• 100% Offline & Private: No accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking. All your flashcards and decks are saved safely on your device.
• Fast & Intuitive: Create questions on the front, answers on the back, and organize cards into customized color-coded decks.
• Smooth 3D Card Flipping: Experience tactile, realistic 3D card flips with instant answer reveals.
• Smart Active Recall: Mark cards as "Got It" or "Need Practice" during study sessions. Drill difficult cards repeatedly until mastered.
• Deck Sharing & Backup: Export any deck as portable JSON to share with classmates via WhatsApp, Messages, or Email, and import decks in seconds.
• Share Study Milestones: Celebrate your recall progress and share study score cards directly to WhatsApp, Stories, and friends.
• Ready-to-Learn Sample Decks: Includes built-in decks for English Vocabulary, Everyday Bangla Essentials, World Capitals, and Computer Science concepts.
• Clean Material 3 Design: Enjoy a modern, responsive layout that supports both Light and Dark themes with zero clutter.

PERFECT FOR:
• Language learning (English, Bangla, Spanish, Japanese, and more)
• School & university exam revision (SAT, GRE, IELTS, BCS, Medical, Engineering)
• Professional certifications and technical interviews
• Daily memory enhancement and trivia practice

Start memorizing with ease today with Flashlet — developed by ViveScript Solutions LLC.
```
