# Privacy Policy for Flashlet

**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

Flashlet is developed and published by **ViveScript Solutions LLC** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, transparent mobile application experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how Flashlet handles your information when you use our Android mobile application.

---

## 1. Summary of Core Principles

- **No User Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to use Flashlet.
- **Local Storage / Offline-First:** All flashcards, study decks, categories, notes, scores, and mastery statistics are stored entirely locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
- **No Data Harvesting:** We do not collect, sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** Flashlet does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, location, or phone storage.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Create (Locally Stored)
When using Flashlet, you create decks and flashcards (such as questions, terms, definitions, and study notes). This content is stored exclusively on your device using Android's sandboxed local application storage (Room/SQLite). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share an exported deck or score using Android's native system share sheet.

### 2.2 Network & Diagnostic Information
Flashlet operates fully offline. If an Internet connection is present, minimal standard network communication may occur strictly for optional advertising components (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to request test advertisements. No user-authored flashcards or study metrics are transmitted over the network.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

### 3.1 Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)
Flashlet includes the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (`com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads:23.6.0`).

- In the current build, Flashlet uses Google's official public test configuration (`ca-app-pub-3940256099942544~3347511713`).
- When advertisements are loaded, Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, and technical network parameters in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve ads and prevent fraudulent activity.
- You can review Google's privacy policies and advertising practices at:  
  [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

Flashlet does not share your flashcard decks, study history, or personal identifiers with advertisers.

---

## 4. Deck Sharing and System Share Sheet

When you choose to use the "Share Deck" or "Share Progress" features:
- Flashlet formats your selected deck or study score into clean text or JSON.
- Flashlet hands this data to Android's native `Intent.ACTION_SEND` system chooser.
- You choose the application (e.g., WhatsApp, Messages, Email, Notes) to receive the data.
- Flashlet has no access to your social media accounts or external contacts.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Flashlet is suitable for general audiences of all ages, including students and learners. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because Flashlet does not collect personal information or require accounts, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your data is stored on your local device:
- You have complete control over your data.
- You can delete any deck or card at any time from within the app.
- Clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently removes all local data from your device.

---

## 7. Security

We take the security of your information seriously. Your data is protected by the Android operating system's application sandbox, preventing other applications from accessing your Flashlet database.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
